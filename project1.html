Electrical Assembly & PCB Design

Summary: Designed an electrical assembly with 3D mechanical layout and corresponding schematic documentation.

Context & Goal

This project focused on designing a heavy-duty firefighting UAV's electrical assembly with clear power and signal interfaces.

My Role

I was responsible for the SolidWorks electrical assembly, schematic capture in KiCad, and system documentation.

Electrical Assembly (3D)

The 3D model shows component placement, mounting, and cable routing with attention to accessibility and strain relief.

What I Learned

Through this project, I learned how electrical assembly design requires balancing power distribution, thermal behavior, and physical layout as a unified system. Studying the UAV parts and power architectures taught me that currents, heat production and the location of components directly impact the device's reliability and ease of servicing.

What I Learned

This project reinforced the importance of coordinating mechanical and electrical design and maintaining clear documentation.

Key Technical Decisions

  • Connector placement optimized for serviceability
  • Clear separation of power and signal wiring
  • Design supports ease of assembly and testing
