Handset & Audio Amplifier PCB Design

Summary: Designed handset and audio amplifier PCBs using industry-standard EDA tools including OrCAD Capture, Allegro PCB Design, and GerbTool.

Context & Goal

This project focused on designing two PCBs — a handset and an audio amplifier — from schematic capture through layout and Gerber file generation, following a professional design workflow.

My Role

I was responsible for schematic capture in OrCAD Capture, PCB layout in Allegro PCB Design, and Gerber file verification in GerbTool.
